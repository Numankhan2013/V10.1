Assumptions & next step (v12). Sample numbers stand in for real study data. The language mirrors the Geist redesign tokens: neutrals, inverse primaries, hairlines, 6–16px radii, Geist + Geist Mono with tabular numerals, 180ms motion, 44px targets, first-class dark mode (toggle above). Motion is transform/opacity-only and fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. If you approve any piece, it can be ported as a narrow change — nothing else moves. Not pushed anywhere; this file is the whole experiment.
